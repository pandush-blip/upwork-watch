# The Upwork Watch

Four solid leads in one morning, from a profile that sat stale for two years. This is the exact setup. A scanner runs twice a day, filtering by money and competition. It dedupes what it has seen. Proposal drafts wait for you before you open Upwork.

- **Twice a day, nothing slips.** It scans jobs posted in the last 2 days. Runs at 07:00 and 16:00 UTC.
- **Filters that matter.** Client rating, spend, verified payment, and competition tier. No sub-$15/hr noise.
- **It remembers.** Every reported job is logged, so you only see what is new.
- **Drafts, not submissions.** Top 3 get ready-to-send cover letters. Nothing fires until the operator approves.

## The shortlist rules (the whole filter)

- Client rating 4.8+ and spend $10K+.
- Verified payment method.
- Competition tier: "Fewer than 5", "5 to 10", or "10 to 15" proposals.
- Expert or intermediate level.
- Skip commission-only, cold-calling-only, and sub-$15/hr gigs unless client spend is very high.

## The loop

1. Scan on schedule.
2. Shortlist and dedupe against everything already reported.
3. Top 3 get drafts, 90-150 words, proof points, one question close.
4. Operator approves. The proposal fires. Connects get logged.
